Help / Tutorial

Basics: Coins accumulate automatically from base income and equipped items/buffs. Equip items by dragging to equipment slots or using the tooltip actions.

Inventory: 6×6 grid. Buffs are 2×2 and must fit. Drag to move. Touch: long-press to open tooltip or start drag.

Shop: 6 item slots + 3 buff slots. Refreshes every 60s. Buy moves item into your inventory (requires space).

Merge: Place exactly 3 items in merge slots and press Merge. You can buy enhancements to shift outcomes toward a higher-value result.

Prestige: Resets most progress but grants permanent base income increases. Use when you can meet the prestige requirement shown.

Tooltips: Hover (desktop) or tap/long-press (mobile) an item to open an interactive tooltip with Equip / Sell actions. Tooltip uses rarity color accent.
